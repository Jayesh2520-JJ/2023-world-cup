Headline Story

19 Nov 2023 | The Final

Australia Silences Ahmedabad: Head's Heroics Seal 6th World Cup Title

Against the odds and a deafening home crowd of over 90,000, Pat Cummins' Australia stunned the unbeaten Indian juggernaut by 6 wickets. Travis Head played the innings of a lifetime, smashing a magnificent 137 to chase down 240 and secure Australia's record-extending sixth ODI World Cup triumph.

Tournament Defining Moments

15 Nov 2023 | Milestone

The Greatest: Kohli Eclipses Tendulkar with Historic 50th Century

In the high-pressure semi-final against New Zealand at Wankhede, Virat Kohli surpassed Sachin Tendulkar's record for most ODI centuries. He bowed to the Master Blaster in the stands after reaching the milestone in a tournament where he scored a record 765 runs.

07 Nov 2023 | The Miracle

"The Greatest ODI Innings Ever Played": Maxwell's One-Legged 201*

Reeling at 91/7 against Afghanistan, Australia looked defeated. Battling severe full-body cramps and unable to run, Glenn Maxwell unleashed an astonishing assault, smashing 201 not out off 128 balls to single-handedly drag Australia into the semi-finals.

Tournament Review | Bowling

The Shami Storm: Benchwarmer to Leading Wicket-Taker

Left out of India's first four matches, Mohammed Shami's return was devastating. He finished as the tournament's leading wicket-taker with 24 scalps at an unbelievable average, including a record-breaking 7/57 in the semi-final against New Zealand.

06 Nov 2023 | Controversy

History & Fury: Angelo Mathews 'Timed Out' in Delhi Drama

In a first for international cricket, Sri Lanka's Angelo Mathews was given 'timed out' after failing to be ready to face the ball within two minutes due to a helmet strap issue against Bangladesh, sparking a massive spirit of cricket debate.

Tournament Review | Underdogs

The Afghan Rise: Toppling Giants and Winning Hearts

Afghanistan enjoyed their best-ever World Cup campaign, securing historic victories over defending champions England, Pakistan, and Sri Lanka. Their spin attack and improved batting made them genuine contenders for a semi-final spot until the final week.

17 Oct 2023 | Upset

Dutch Courage: Netherlands Stun High-Flying South Africa

In one of the shocks of the tournament at Dharamsala, the Netherlands, led by Scott Edwards, defended a modest total to defeat the in-form South African side by 38 runs in a rain-curtailed encounter.
